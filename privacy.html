Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

RumahKira (“the app”) is developed by Yong Hing Wong. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no account sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Information stored on your device

The numbers and remarks you enter (such as property price, loan amount, interest rate, age and saved comparisons) and your light/dark mode choice are saved only on your iPhone so the app can remember them. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it. Deleting the app removes it.

3. Sharing

When you choose to share a result (as an image, text, PDF or via WhatsApp), the app hands the content to the app or service you select using the standard iOS share options. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy. The app only copies text to your clipboard when you tap “Copy for WhatsApp”.

4. Internet access

The app works fully offline and does not send your data to any server.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
